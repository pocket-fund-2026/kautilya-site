Contact

Write to hello@pocket-fund.com with your phone model, the app version (Profile, at the bottom) and what happened. We reply by email.

Signing in

This version runs entirely on your phone. Enter any 10-digit mobile number and the practice sign-in code shown on the screen (123456). No text message is sent. To look around as a vendor, use the sample vendor number shown on the sign-in screen (9000000001). Different phone numbers are separate profiles on the same phone.

What this version does and does not do

  • Vendor listings are fictional samples, so enquiries and bookings are practice only.
  • Payments, escrow and Booking Protection are simulated. No real money moves.
  • Your data stays on your phone and does not sync between phones. Photo uploads, the shareable wedding website and RSVP links need an online service and are not part of this version.

Deleting your data

Open Profile and choose "Delete my account data", or delete the app. See the privacy policy for details.
