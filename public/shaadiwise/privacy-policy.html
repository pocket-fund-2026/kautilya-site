Privacy Policy

ShaadiWise (iPhone and Android) · Publisher: Jigsaw Ventures LLC · Effective 12 November 2026 · Applies to version 1.0 and later until this page says otherwise

The short version

This version of ShaadiWise keeps everything on your phone. It has no server of ours behind it, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send what you enter to us or to anyone else. We do not know who you are.

What you enter and where it stays

When you sign up, the app asks for a name and a 10-digit phone number, and it asks whether you are a couple or a vendor. As you use the app you may add wedding details such as dates, events, a budget, a checklist, guests (names and phone numbers), vendor shortlists, enquiries, messages, notes and your language choice. All of this is saved in a database inside the app on your phone. Nothing in this version uploads it.

The phone number is used only to separate profiles on the same phone. The sign-in code in this version is a fixed practice code shown on the sign-in screen, and no text message is sent. Vendor listings you see are fictional samples.

What leaves your phone, and only when you tap

  • WhatsApp and other apps. If you tap an invite or share button, the app opens WhatsApp or the iOS or Android share sheet with a message already written. You choose the recipient and whether to send. WhatsApp and the app you pick handle that message under their own privacy policies.
  • Links. If you open a link (for example a vendor's website), your browser or the linked app opens, and that site can see your IP address like any website. You can also paste the address of a photo that is already online, for a vendor portfolio or an ideas board. The app then loads that picture from its address, which lets the site hosting it see your IP address.
  • Export. "Export my data" in Profile hands a copy of your data to the share sheet. It goes only where you send it.

Notifications

If you switch on reminders, the app asks permission and then schedules notifications on your phone for tasks that are due. They are created and shown on your phone and are not sent through any server.

Photos

This version does not read your photo library or your camera, and it does not upload photographs.

What we do not collect

No account on our servers, no email address, no location, no contacts, no advertising identifier, no analytics software and no third-party trackers. Because the data stays on your phone, we cannot see it, recover it or delete it for you.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Profile and choose "Delete my account data". This removes your profile, wedding plans and messages from the app, and signs you out. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Your phone's own backups, for example iCloud Backup or Google backup if you have them switched on, may include app data. Those backups are governed by Apple's or Google's terms and your settings, not by us. We cannot delete copies you have already shared with other people or apps.

Payments

This version does not process real payments. Payment, escrow and "Booking Protection" screens are simulations, and no card, bank or UPI details are collected or sent.

Children

ShaadiWise is a wedding-planning tool for adults and is not directed at children. It collects nothing that could identify anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, for example by adding an online service, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change too.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@pocket-fund.com. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you choose to write, and we use it only to reply. You can ask us to delete that correspondence by writing to the same address.
