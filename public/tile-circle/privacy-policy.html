Privacy Policy

Tile Circle (iPhone, Android and its web link pages) · Publisher: Jigsaw Ventures LLC · Effective 28 November 2026 · Applies to version 1.0 and later until this page says otherwise

The short version

Tile Circle helps people who play mahjong with real tiles arrange their games, classes and events. You need an account. We keep what the app needs to do that. We do not sell your information, we show no advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. This first release is open only to invited testers, and it contains made-up sample circles and events.

What we keep

  • Your account: your name and mobile number. Your mobile number is how you sign in.
  • If you sign in with Apple or Google: Google's Firebase Authentication service, which handles sign-in for us, keeps the email address (and the name, if given) that Apple or Google pass to it. With Apple you can choose to hide your email address. Our own server does not read that email address.
  • What you do in the app: your circles, games, replies, waitlist places, the places (1st to 4th) entered after a game, season points and your skill tier, titles, class bookings and attendance, event and team entries, and short notes you write, such as a note with a seat request.
  • Addresses: the address you enter if you host a game or run a venue.
  • Bookings: tickets, class places, receipts and invoices. In this first release the ticket payment page is a test page: no money is taken and no card or bank details are asked for.
  • Notifications: if you switch them on, a device token that lets us send notices to your phone.
  • App diagnostics: crash reports, and which kinds of screens are opened. Collection is off until you sign in. We do not use an advertising identifier, and ad personalisation is switched off.
  • Technical records: our server notes each request (the page asked for, the result and how long it took). The services that carry the traffic may also record network details such as your IP address.

The app does not ask for your location, your contacts, your camera or your microphone.

Why we use it

To sign you in, run your circles, games, classes and events, send you notices you need, keep the records the law asks for, keep the app safe, and find and fix faults.

Who can see it

  • Your mobile number is not shown to other players. The one exception: the organizer of a class you book sees your number, and the booking page says so.
  • A host's home address is shown only to players who hold a seat at that game.
  • Boards are seen only inside your circle. Your tier is visible only to you unless you choose otherwise.
  • Your name on public tournament results and appearing in event photos are off unless you say yes.
  • Other players in your circles, hosts and event staff see your name (a first name and last initial) and what you have said you will attend.

Companies that handle information for us

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Analytics, and Google Cloud, where our server runs in Mumbai, India). To check that a phone sign-in comes from a real person, Google may show its reCAPTCHA check, which collects device and browser information under Google's privacy policy.
  • Vercel, which passes the app's traffic on to our server.
  • Apple, for Sign in with Apple and for delivering notifications to iPhones.

These companies may process information outside India. We may also disclose information when the law requires it. WhatsApp messaging and a payment company are not connected in this release.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your information while you have an account. You can download your data, or delete your account, in the app under Me. Deleting your account removes your name and mobile number from our records, ends your sign-ins, removes your device tokens and takes you out of upcoming games. Past game records stay in your circles without your name. Tickets, invoices and receipts are kept for as long as the law requires. Copies in our server backups are removed within 14 days. See Delete your account for how to ask us to delete it, including your sign-in record with Google.

Age

Tile Circle is for people aged 18 and over.

Your rights and our contact

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, withdraw consent, or make a complaint, by writing to hello@pocket-fund.com. We will answer within 30 days.
