Tile Circle Support

For the iPhone and Android app · Publisher: Jigsaw Ventures LLC

Contact us

Write to hello@pocket-fund.com. Please say which phone you use and what happened. Do not send us a sign-in code.

Who can sign in

This first release is open only to invited testers, with Indian mobile numbers. If the app says it is in a private test, your number has not been invited yet. Ask the person who invited you, or write to us.

Signing in

  • Type your mobile number and tap Send code. A short "are you a person" check from Google may appear first.
  • On iPhone you can also use Apple or Google, then add your mobile number once.

What this release does not do yet

The circles and events you see are samples. Ticket payments are a test page, so no money moves. Messages by WhatsApp are not sent yet.

Reminders

You can switch reminders on or off under Me, then Privacy.

Reporting something

If a note, a name or anything else in the app is offensive or wrong, write to hello@pocket-fund.com with what you saw and where. We will look into it and remove it if needed.

Your data

Under Me you can download your data or delete your account. See also our privacy policy.
