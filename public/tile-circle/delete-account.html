Delete your Tile Circle account

App: Tile Circle · Publisher: Jigsaw Ventures LLC

In the app

  1. Open Tile Circle and sign in.
  2. Tap Me.
  3. Under Your data, tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Without the app

Write to hello@pocket-fund.com with the subject "Delete my Tile Circle account" and the mobile number on the account. We may ask you to confirm that the number is yours. We will delete the account within 30 days and tell you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your name and mobile number, your sign-ins and device tokens, your seats in upcoming games, your messages waiting to be sent, and your titles.
  • Your sign-in record with Google's Firebase Authentication, including any email address from Apple or Google. The app removes it when you delete your account there. If it could not, or you want us to check, write to us.

What is kept

  • Past game records stay in your circles without your name.
  • Tickets, invoices and receipts are kept for as long as the law requires.
  • Copies in server backups are removed within 14 days.
