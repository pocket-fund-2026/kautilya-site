Privacy Policy

WW2: Turning Point (Android) · Last updated: 27 October 2026 · Publisher: Jigsaw Ventures LLC

This is an offline single-player strategy game. No account is required. This release has no advertising, in-app purchases, analytics SDK, tracking SDK or remote AI service. We do not operate a gameplay server or transmit your campaign progress or personal information to our servers.

Campaign progress, preferences and story choices are stored on your device. Clear the app storage in Android Settings to delete local progress and settings. Uninstalling removes local app data, subject to Android backup and restore settings.

Android and Google Play may process installation, diagnostic or backup information under their own policies and your device settings. The app permits Android-managed backups. Platform-provided diagnostics may be made available to the developer. This is separate from an analytics service operated by us.

When you choose to export or share, you select the receiving app and content. That app applies its own privacy practices. Historical-source links open third-party websites only when selected. They are not necessary to play.

The app requests Internet access for its WebView and platform functionality, and vibration permission for optional haptics. It does not request advertising ID, camera, microphone or location access. Game maps and story files are bundled locally.

The game is intended for older teens and adults. There is no public player feed or messaging service. If you contact us for support, we receive what you voluntarily include and use it to respond. Avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

Privacy inquiries: open this game’s Google Play listing, expand App support, and use the developer contact details published there. We will update this policy if our practices change.
