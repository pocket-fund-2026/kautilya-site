Privacy Policy

Food Jasoos (iPhone) · Publisher: Jigsaw Ventures LLC · Effective 12 November 2026 · Applies to version 1.0 and later until this page says otherwise

The short version

Food Jasoos has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your iPhone reads the text off a food pack itself. To judge the label, the app sends that text, and where each piece of text sat on the pack, to our service. Photographs of a pack leave your phone only when the phone could not read enough from it. We do not know who you are.

What the app sends, and when

Label text and layout. When you scan a pack, Apple's text recognition on your iPhone reads the label. The app sends the recognised text and its layout to our assessment service, hosted on Vercel, which works out what the label means and sends a verdict back. If the app read a barcode on the pack, that barcode goes with it, so that a suggested alternative is never the pack in your hand. We do not store anything against the barcode.

Photographs, only when needed. Sometimes the phone cannot read enough off a pack, for example small print or a curved or shiny packet. When that happens, the app sends the photographs of that pack to our service, which passes them to Google's Gemini model for a closer reading. This happens automatically, without a further prompt. The scan screen says so, and the verdict says whether photographs were sent. Our service does not keep the photographs. Google processes them under its Gemini API terms, which may allow Google to keep inputs for a limited time for abuse monitoring.

Plain-language summary. When you choose "Explain this result", the app sends the text it read off the pack and our findings about it, never a photograph, to Google's Gemini model, which writes a short summary. If you have switched on a personal view (watching blood sugar, watching blood pressure, or buying for a child), the names of the views you chose go with the request so the summary can speak to them. These views are your own choice and are guidelines, not health advice. Our service does not keep them.

Hindi. If you choose Hindi, the words of the verdict are translated into Hindi by Google's Gemini model. Only words are sent, never a photograph.

Pack vs Pitch. If you import a screenshot there, the screenshot is read on your phone. Only the words you confirm, together with the pack's findings, go to our service to be checked. The image itself is not sent.

Barcode lookup. If you look up a barcode that the bundled directory does not have, the barcode is sent from your phone to Open Food Facts, a public food database, which can see your phone's IP address like any website. We do not add anything else to that request.

Reviews you choose to send. "Send this scan for review" is your own tap. It sends the text the app read, the figures taken from it, the verdict's headline answers and any note you type, never a photograph. Please do not put personal information in the note. Our hosting platform records these in its logs, which it keeps for as long as it retains logs. We do not keep them anywhere else.

Questions you nominate. Questions saved under Investigate next stay on your phone. If the app offers to submit one to us, it does so only after you agree and tap Submit, and it sends the product name, your question and an optional source link.

What we do not collect

No account, no name, no email address, no phone number, no location and no contacts. No advertising identifier, no analytics software and no third-party trackers.

Each request from the app carries one random number that the app made the first time it ran. It is not your phone's identifier and is not made from anything about you or your phone. It is used for one thing: so that one phone cannot use up the closer readings, summaries and translations for everyone. It is kept in the app's storage on your phone, so deleting the app deletes it. Our service holds it in memory only and does not write it down.

Camera and photos

The app uses your camera to photograph packs, and asks permission first. To use a photo you already have, you choose it in the iOS photo picker. The app sees only the photos you choose, not your library. Ordinary scans do not save their photographs.

What stays on your device

  • your scan register: the label readings, findings and any saved explanation;
  • your shopping list, prices, household notes, personal views and language choice;
  • questions you saved under Investigate next;
  • label photographs you deliberately save as evidence in Label changes. They are stored only on your phone, excluded from backup, and limited to 20 photos or 40 MB. You can delete them one by one or all at once.

Scan diagnostics, a developer tool that can store pack photographs locally, is switched off in the App Store version. None of this data is uploaded except as described above. Deleting the app deletes it. Your device's own backups, for example iCloud Backup if you have it switched on, may include app data. Those backups are governed by Apple's terms and your settings, not by us.

Sharing

Sharing a case file or a question opens the iOS share sheet. You choose the recipient. A case file carries finding text, quoted label evidence, source citations and unknowns, and a photograph only if you add one. A copy goes to the app or person you pick, and deleting a local record or the app does not delete copies you have already shared.

Hosting and service providers

Our assessment service runs on Vercel. Hosting infrastructure necessarily processes technical request information such as IP addresses, request times and response status, and keeps operational logs under its configuration and provider practices. These logs are used to run, diagnose and protect the service, not for advertising. Our service also writes operational log lines, for example whether a closer reading was used and how many tokens it took. These lines do not include photographs.

The service providers involved are Vercel (hosting), Google (the Gemini model), and Open Food Facts (barcode information). Each has its own privacy policy, and they may process information in countries other than yours. Apple may collect data about downloads and crashes under its own privacy policy and your device settings.

Health and food information

Food Jasoos explains what is printed on a pack. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Readings from the phone or from an AI model can be wrong, so check important details on the pack. Ask a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

Children

The app is intended for adult shoppers and is not directed at children. It collects nothing that could identify anyone, including children.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can delete individual scans, saved label photographs and household notes inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything on your phone. We hold no account or profile about you, so there is nothing to look up. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you choose to write, and we use it to reply. You can ask us to delete that correspondence by writing to the address below. We cannot recover or delete records that exist only on your phone.

Changes
