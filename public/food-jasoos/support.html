How to use it (iPhone)

  1. Scan. Open the Scan tab and photograph the front of a pack, its ingredient list and its nutrition table, or choose photos you already have. English and Hindi labels are read on your phone.
  2. Read the verdict. The app judges the printed label and shows what it found and what it could not tell. Choose "Explain this result" for a short plain-language summary.
  3. Look things up. Discover holds food records with their sources, and you can look up a barcode. Cases keeps your saved scans. Method explains how the app works and holds the privacy and language settings.

Questions

Does the app need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. Scanning a pack needs an internet connection because the label is judged by our service. Your saved scans stay on your phone.

Are my photos uploaded?

Your phone reads the label first and sends only the text it read and where it sat. Photographs are sent for a closer reading only when the phone could not read enough, and the verdict tells you when that happened. Ordinary scans do not save photographs. See the privacy policy.

Can I switch to Hindi?

Yes. Choose the language in the Method tab. Verdict words are then shown in Hindi.

Can I use it in landscape?

No. The iPhone app plays in portrait only.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual scans, saved label photographs and household notes inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your phone. We keep no account or profile about you.

The label reading looks wrong.

Retake the photo closer and straight on, with the shine off the pack. You can also choose "Send this scan for review" on a verdict to tell us, or email us.

How to use it (Android)

Use Scan to photograph a food pack, choose a label photo or paste label text. Include the front, ingredients and nutrition panel. English and Hindi recognition runs on the phone. Online assessment sends text first; sending photos for a closer AI reading requires a separate confirmation.

Discover contains attributed food records. They are source declarations, not laboratory measurements or a guarantee about a current pack. List stores your shopping items and entered prices. Register opens saved assessment cases. Local records work offline; online assessments and public barcode lookup need internet access.

On Android, Method, privacy and language is available from the question-mark button and includes local deletion controls. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local records. Shared copies are controlled by their recipient.

Important limits

Text recognition and AI readings may be wrong. Verify important label details yourself. Discover records are what the manufacturer or source declared, not laboratory measurements.
