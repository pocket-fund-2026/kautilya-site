How to use it

  1. Set up. Choose a language, then enter the name of the person who will use the app and, if you like, a family member's name and phone number. Allow notifications when the app asks.
  2. Add a medicine. Type it in, or tap the microphone and say it, for example "Telma 40, morning and night". Sambhal shows what it understood. Check the name and the times, then confirm. If it is not sure of a time it asks you instead of guessing.
  3. Get reminded. At each medicine time the phone shows a notification. Tap it to open a large full-screen reminder that reads the medicine names aloud, then tap to say you have taken it, or to put it off for a while.
  4. Call family. If you added a family member's number, a button on the home screen opens the phone's dialer with it.

Questions

Does the app need an account or an internet connection?

No account and no sign-in. The app keeps everything on your phone and works without an internet connection. Speaking a medicine uses your phone's own speech recognition, which may need a connection on some phones.

My reminder came late, or did not come.

Please check that notifications are allowed for Sambhal in your phone's settings and that the phone is not in a mode that silences them. On Android, battery saver or "optimise battery" settings can delay reminders, and a reminder can arrive a few minutes after the set time. On some phones you can set Sambhal to "Unrestricted" battery use to help. If the phone is switched off at the medicine time, the reminder cannot show. For medicines that matter, please do not rely on any single reminder.

The app did not understand what I said.

Speak slowly and clearly in the language you chose, and say the medicine name and the time together, for example "Metformin 500, morning and night". The app will tell you when it did not understand, and you can always type the medicine instead. A time like "8 o'clock" with no morning or evening, or a phrase such as "twice a day", is not turned into a time for you. The app asks you to pick the times.

Which languages does it support?

Hindi, English, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam and Punjabi. Some phones do not have a voice for every language, so the spoken reminder may fall back to a chime and the text on screen. If a word in your language looks wrong, please write to us and we will fix it.

How do I delete my data?

Delete medicines and recordings inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it keeps on your phone. There is no account or server copy. See the privacy policy.

I changed phones. Where did my medicines go?

The app keeps your information only on the phone it is installed on, so a new phone starts empty. Please add your medicines again on the new phone.

Important limits

Sambhal is a reminder. It does not give medical advice, check doses or interactions, or know whether a medicine is right for you. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Speech recognition can make mistakes, so always check the medicine name and times on the confirmation screen before you save. Follow your doctor's instructions, and ask a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.
