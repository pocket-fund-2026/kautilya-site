Privacy Policy

Sambhal: Medicine Reminder (iPhone and Android) · Publisher: Jigsaw Ventures LLC · Effective 18 November 2026 · Applies to version 1.0 and later until this page says otherwise

The short version

Sambhal has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It has no server. Everything you enter, such as medicine names, reminder times and family details, is kept on your own phone, and the app does not send it to us or to anyone else. We do not know who you are.

What the app keeps on your phone

  • the names and phone numbers you type in for the person using the app and for a family member;
  • the medicines you add, their notes and their reminder times;
  • a record of which reminders were confirmed, put off or missed;
  • your language choice and settings;
  • short voice recordings, only if a family member chooses to record a spoken reminder for a medicine.

None of this is uploaded. Deleting a medicine removes it from the app, and deleting the app from your phone removes everything it kept. On iPhone, your device's own backups, for example iCloud Backup if you have it switched on, may include app data. Those backups are governed by Apple's terms and your settings, not by us. The Android app switches Android's automatic app backup off.

Microphone and speech

The app asks for the microphone only for two things, and only when you tap the button: speaking a medicine to add it, and recording a spoken reminder.

Speaking a medicine. When you tap the microphone, your phone's own speech recognition turns what you say into text. On iPhone this is Apple's speech recognition, and on Android it is the phone's speech service, which is normally Google's. These services may send your voice to their servers to be turned into text, under Apple's or Google's own terms and privacy policies. Sambhal itself only receives the text. It does not keep the audio and it does not send the text anywhere. It reads the text on your phone and shows you what it understood, and nothing is saved until you confirm it.

Recording a spoken reminder. If a family member records their voice for a medicine, the recording is stored only on your phone and plays at the medicine time. It is not uploaded. You can remove it at any time.

You can say no to the microphone and speech permissions and still use the app by typing.

Notifications

The app asks to show notifications so it can remind you at the times you set. These are scheduled on your phone itself. No notification service of ours is involved, and nothing is sent through a server.

Phone calls

If you add a family member's phone number, the app can show a button that opens your phone's dialer with that number. The app does not place the call itself and does not send the number anywhere.

What we do not collect

No account, no email address, no location, no contacts, no photos, no advertising identifier, no analytics software and no third-party trackers. Because the app keeps your information on your phone and has no server, we hold no record of it and cannot look it up, read it or recover it.

Messages to other people

Sambhal does not send messages, texts or alerts to anyone else's phone. Anything a family member sees is shown on the phone that holds the data.

Health information

Sambhal is a reminder. It does not give medical advice, does not check doses or interactions, and does not know whether a medicine is right for you. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. A reminder can be late or missed, for example if the phone is switched off, notifications are turned off, or the phone's battery saver delays them, and on Android a reminder can arrive a few minutes after the set time. Please do not rely on the app alone for medicines that matter. Follow your doctor's instructions, and ask a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

Children

Sambhal is intended for adults and the families who look after them. It is not directed at children, and it collects nothing from anyone.

Service providers

We use no analytics, advertising or hosting services for this app. The only outside services involved are the ones built into your phone, namely Apple's or Google's speech recognition if you use the microphone, and the operating system's notifications. Apple and Google may collect data about downloads and crashes under their own privacy policies and your device settings.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can delete medicines and recordings inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything on your phone. We hold no account or profile about you, so there is nothing for us to look up or delete. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you choose to write, and we use it to reply. You can ask us to delete that correspondence by writing to the address below.
