What VegHai does

VegHai is for Indian vegetarians travelling abroad. It does three jobs: it checks a food label against your own food rules, it shows restaurant staff a card in their language, and it helps you choose the right special-meal code on a flight.

What the answers mean

Doesn’t fit. A word printed on the pack breaks one of your rules. VegHai shows that exact word in its own script, what it means, and which rule it breaks.

Can’t tell. The label does not settle the question. An ingredient may come from a plant or an animal (E471, “natural flavour”, shortening), or VegHai has no checked entry for it yet. VegHai says which, and gives you a question to ask.

Not read. Not enough was read to say, for example because there was no ingredient list in the photo.

Why VegHai does not say “Fits” yet

A “Fits” answer needs the whole ingredient list read, two readings that agree, and every ingredient matched to an entry that a named person has checked. The ingredient entries in VegHai 1.0 are still being checked by people. Until an entry has been checked, VegHai will not use it to say that something fits. So even a plain list such as rice, sugar and salt shows as “Can’t tell”, with the note that the entry is not yet checked. That is on purpose. A wrong “Fits” is the one mistake the app must not make.

What a label cannot show

A label does not say what else was fried in the same oil, how a wine was fined, whether sugar was refined with bone char, or what a kitchen did with a dish. Menus never get a green mark for the same reason. Going by the label only is going by the label only.

Not for allergies

VegHai is not for allergies and makes no medical claims. If an allergy is the reason you are checking food, ask the people who made it.

Common questions

How do I report a wrong answer?

Use “This looks wrong” on the answer screen. It opens your mail app with a message already written, and nothing is sent until you press send. You can also write to the address above. We read the reports we receive and use them to correct the data.

Is the card correct?

The card sentences are written in plain English first and then translated. The translations are machine drafts that native speakers are reviewing. A card says “Machine translation. Not yet checked by a native speaker.” at its foot until a person has checked that language. “Show me the English” lets you see exactly what you are showing.

Flight meals

The meal codes and cut-off times are gathered from what each airline publishes, and the app shows when each entry was last read. Airlines change these. Check the airline’s booking page when you book, and ask at check-in for the meal to be read back to you. Meal requests go to the airline that flies the plane.

Does it work without a signal?

The card, the questions for the kitchen, flight meals, the ingredient lookup and label reading work with no signal. The reading model is downloaded once, which needs a connection. Barcode lookups need a connection too, because they ask Open Food Facts.

VegHai could not read my label

Reading depends on the script, the light and the phone. Try again with the list flat, in even light, and filling the frame, or use “Type it in”. Some phones cannot read every script by themselves. When VegHai cannot read a label it says so. It does not guess.

Where is my data, and how do I delete it?

Your food rules, trips and saved answers are on your phone only. Deleting the app deletes them. The privacy policy lists every connection the app makes.

Is there a paid version?

Not in 1.0. Everything in the app is free. A trip pass is planned for a later version, and the app says so.
