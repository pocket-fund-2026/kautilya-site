Client · Multi-store retail

Furtados Music

Twelve stores, five different accounting systems, and one nightly ritual: retype every number by hand and hope nothing's off. We built the software that ends it, one inbox in, one honest summary out, running quietly on Furtados's own server before the next store opens.

Sector: Musical-instruments retail Engagement: Store-intelligence bot Status: ◐ In progress Built with: AI · runs on their own server

A walkthrough of the system we built for Furtados, from the nightly inbox to the summary that's waiting on the desk before the store opens.

01

The problem

Furtados Music runs a chain of stores, and each one closes its day on whatever accounting system it uses, Tally, Busy, Zoho, Marg or plain Excel. Every night, head office received a stack of mismatched Excel, CSV and PDF reports and reconciled them by hand, hours of manual work each night, with mistakes and odd numbers only surfacing at month-end, long after they mattered.

  • Every store on a different accounting system and export format
  • Hours of manual reconciliation by hand, every night
  • Mistakes and anomalies caught late, at month-end, not overnight

Formats in play

Tally Busy Zoho Marg Excel / CSV / PDF
02

What we built

The solution: custom software that collects every store's nightly report automatically from one inbox, reads it no matter what format it arrives in, and merges it into a single clean view. AI makes sense of the messy, off-template reports and explains anything unusual in plain English. Each store is checked against its own normal, and anything off is flagged, then one clear summary email lands before the next business day.

  • Every store's nightly report is collected automatically from one inbox, nobody has to chase them
  • Reports in any format, Excel, CSV or PDF, are read and merged into one clean view
  • AI reads the messy, off-template reports and explains anything unusual in plain English
  • Each store is checked against its own normal, and anything off, a cash gap, a missing report, an odd expense, is flagged
  • One clear summary email lands before the next business day; new stores slot in without extra setup
