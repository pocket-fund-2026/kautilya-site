Client · Luxury fashion

JADE by Monica & Karishma

Designing, making and selling couture under one roof meant work was scattered everywhere, no single view of what was being made, production tracked on the side, and customers left waiting on WhatsApp. We built custom software to fix each part: one system to run collections, one to run production, and an assistant that answers customers on WhatsApp.

Sector: Couture & bridal · Mumbai Engagement: Custom software, built around how they work Status: Live Built with: web apps · AI · cloud
01

The system that runs collections

Live

The problem: designs, samples, suppliers and to-dos lived in different places, notebooks, chats and people's heads. Nobody had one view of what was being made or where it stood, so pieces stalled and things slipped.

What we built: one place to run every collection, from first sketch to finished piece, on simple shared boards the whole studio can see. Everyone knows what's being made, who's doing it and what's next.

  • Track each piece from sketch to sample to production to marketing, all in one view
  • Keep suppliers, samples and orders next to the designs they belong to
  • Assign work, set targets and get a daily nudge on anything overdue
  • Each team sees only its own work, so the studio stays organised

02

The system that runs production

◐ Rolling out

The problem: making a garment meant juggling orders, costing, work sent out to the karigar workshops, materials and quality checks across separate registers, so orders started before they were properly priced, and it was hard to say where any piece stood.

What we built: the system that runs the making of each garment, in one connected flow. Every order is priced and approved before work starts, and each piece is tracked all the way to done.

  • Turn a customer order into a costed, approved job before any work begins
  • Send work out to the karigar workshops and track the materials that go with it
  • Run quality checks on every piece, then move it into finished goods and bill it
  • See where every order stands with clear reports on progress, timing and quality

03

Tina, the WhatsApp assistant

Live

The problem: most enquiries come in on WhatsApp, and customers were left waiting for someone free to reply, questions about occasion, budget and what's available piled up, and interest cooled before anyone could answer.

What we built: Tina, JADE's WhatsApp assistant. She answers the store's WhatsApp, understands the occasion and budget, suggests real pieces at real prices, and can even match a customer's photo to the exact outfit, then hands over to a person to close the sale. She also helps staff create and track to-dos by text.

  • Answers the store's WhatsApp and understands the occasion and budget
  • Suggests real pieces at real prices, so customers see what they can actually buy
  • Match a customer's photo to the exact outfit in the collection
  • Hands over to a person the moment a customer is ready to buy, and helps staff track to-dos by text
