JADECouture software suiteClient · Live suite
Luxury fashion · couture & bridal · JADE by Monica & Karishma

The problem

JADE designs, makes and sells couture under one roof, but the collection, the making of each piece and the customer conversations all lived in separate places, so nothing joined up and too much depended on people remembering.

The custom software we built & run

  • Fashion CRM. One place to run every collection from first sketch to finished piece: designs, production boards you drag and drop, suppliers and samples, and clear targets for each person.
  • Order & production system. Follows a piece through the whole making process: order, pricing, work sent to tailors, materials, quality checks, finished goods and billing, all in one flow.
  • Tina, a WhatsApp AI assistant. Answers customers, recommends real pieces at real prices, matches a photo to the exact piece, and hands over to a person when needed, plus helps staff with their tasks.

Furtados MusicStore Intelligence Bot◐ In progress
Multi-store retail · musical instruments · Mumbai

The problem

A chain of stores each sent its nightly numbers in a different format, Excel, spreadsheets, PDFs, and someone had to pull them together by hand every morning, so problems only surfaced late.

The custom software we built

  • Each store's nightly report is collected automatically from one shared inbox, every night.
  • Mixed Excel, spreadsheet and PDF reports are tidied into one consistent format.
  • AI reads even off-template reports and explains anything unusual in plain language.
  • Each store is compared against its own recent average, so odd numbers get flagged.
  • One clear summary email lands before the next day opens; adding a new store is a quick setting change.

  • Their data stays puton their own systems, no SaaS lock-in
